# Sales, customer success and growth (CRM / GRW)

## Feedback and growth (GRW)

| ID | L | Requirement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| GRW-01 | 2 | NPS at D+30 of paying and every 90 days (Formbricks / own) |
| GRW-02 | 2 | Testimonials and case studies collected and published |
| GRW-03 | 3 | Referral program (credit for referrer and referee) |
| GRW-04 | 3 | Affiliate/partner program |

## Sales and customer success (CRM)

| ID | L | Requirement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CRM-01 | 1 | One CRM is the source of truth; signup/lead creates contact + company automatically (no spreadsheets) |
| CRM-02 | 1 | Pipeline stages have written exit criteria; every deal has owner, next step and date |
| CRM-03 | 1 | Activation event tracked per new customer with an onboarding checklist (ONB-01) |
| CRM-04 | 1 | CRM token used by agents/MCP is scoped (read + limited write), never a super-admin key |
| CRM-05 | 2 | Health score per account (usage, active seats, tickets, payment status) reviewed weekly |
| CRM-06 | 2 | Churn-risk triggers (usage drop, failed payment, champion left) create a task automatically |
| CRM-07 | 2 | Two-way sync: product events → CRM, plan/MRR from billing → CRM; win/loss and churn reasons as structured fields |
| CRM-08 | 3 | Renewal and expansion playbooks, QBRs for top accounts, NPS/CSAT feeding health score, lead scoring and routing |

Reference: [GitLab customer health scoring](https://handbook.gitlab.com/handbook/customer-success/customer-health-scoring/).
